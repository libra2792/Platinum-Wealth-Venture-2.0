Welcome Bonus and Investment Bonus are quoted on the annual premium, so the three columns are not like-for-like at a glance. Over four years the Investment Bonus adds up to 2% of total basic premiums on every option (4 × 0.5% of one premium · 4 × 1% of one of two · 4 × 2.5% of one of five). Performance Bonus and Supplementary Charge are both worked on Basic Premium Policy Value.

🛡️
Protection
Death Benefit
Higher of: (a) the single premium or total regular premiums paid + top-ups – withdrawals, or (b) policy value — less any amounts owing. Accidental death within the first 2 policy years: an additional 100% of the single premium, or of total regular premiums paid (up to 2 policy years of premiums).
⭐
Loyalty
Performance Bonus
0.40% p.a. of Basic Premium Policy Value every year from the start of Year 8, on all three premium terms — paid while the policy is in force and, for 2-Pay and 5-Pay, all regular premiums have been fully paid.
👨‍👩‍👧
Legacy Planning
Secondary Insured
Name yourself, your spouse or a child under 16. Upon the insured's death the policy continues under the Secondary Insured — no payout, no disruption, subject to AIA's acceptance. Max appointment age 75. Cash plans only: not available on an SRS policy, or alongside a nomination or trust.
🔄
Anytime
Fund Flexibility
A wide range of AIA ILP sub-funds, AIA Elite Funds and AIA Guided Portfolios. Switch, rebalance or auto-switch at any time, free of charge. GAIF/GDIF dividends from the next payout date.
✅
Simple to Start
Guaranteed Issuance
No medical underwriting on any premium term. Cash plans accept an insured from 2 weeks old to age 75; the SRS single premium plan from age 18 to 75.
➕
Add More Later
Top-Ups
From S$1,000, any time the policy is in force and regular premiums are up to date. A 3% premium charge applies; top-up units carry no supplementary, surrender or partial withdrawal charge.

Early surrender is costly.

Partial Withdrawal Charge

Benefit Charge
Monthly cost of the death cover, charged only on the sum-at-risk: premiums paid + top-ups – withdrawals – policy value. Nothing is charged in a month where the policy value is at or above that figure. The rate follows the insured's age and gender.
