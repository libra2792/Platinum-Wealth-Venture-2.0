§ 06
Is This Plan Right for You?
Find your fit — honest guidance on who this plan serves best
▾

🎯 This plan is made for you if…

You have S$18,000–S$42,000+ per year to deploy with purpose. The full welcome bonus potential unlocks at S$42,000 annually — this plan was built for investors who can commit at that level and want their capital working from day one.
You want to be done paying in five years and let the policy run. A shorter, sharper premium commitment appeals to higher earners who prefer to front-load their obligation and then step back. Five years, then freedom.
You're building wealth with a 10–20 year view — funding retirement income, creating a legacy, or building a dividend stream. The bonuses in years 8–11 are meaningfully sized at this premium level.
You prefer the supplementary charge to end sooner. At 7 years higher-premium investors benefit more from the shorter drag window — more of your returns compound unimpeded, sooner.
You want dividend income without waiting. With GAIF or GDIF, dividends can start flowing from the very next payout date — no initial investment period to clear first.

💡 You might want to explore alternatives if…

S$18,000 per year is a stretch right now. This plan's lowest tier (S$18K–S$23,999) carries zero welcome bonus — the plan truly shines at S$42,000+.
You'd prefer a longer, more gradual commitment. If paying S$18,000+ for five consecutive years feels concentrated an alternative plan with a longer premium schedule may spread the obligation more comfortably.
You need your capital to remain accessible within the first 7 years. Surrender charges are meaningful through Year 7. This plan rewards those who truly set it and forget it — if there's a chance you'll need liquidity before then, plan your emergency reserves accordingly first.
You're looking for guaranteed capital protection. As an ILP, policy value moves with fund performance. The structure is designed to grow over the long term, but no return is guaranteed and market fluctuations are part of the journey.

The bottom line: PWV 2.0 is the high-conviction plan for affluent investors who want a clean five-year obligation, a faster end to supplementary charges, and a higher performance bonus rate from year eight. At S$42,000+, the welcome bonus adds meaningful momentum. At S$18,000–S$24,000, the plan still works well. The right choice depends entirely on what you can deploy comfortably and consistently.
